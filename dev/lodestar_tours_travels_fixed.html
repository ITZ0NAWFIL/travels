From first call to landing

Five stages, always in this order. You see the status of each one before we move to the next.

  1. Profile

    We record your skills, experience, passport and the country you want to work in.

  2. Employer match

    Your CV goes only to employers with a confirmed vacancy. You interview with them directly.

  3. Offer & contract

    You get the offer letter and contract in writing, in a language you read, before you pay any fee.

  4. Visa & clearances

    Visa file, medical, police clearance and emigration paperwork, tracked line by line.

  5. Flight & arrival

    Ticket, pre-departure briefing and a contact at the other end for your first week.
